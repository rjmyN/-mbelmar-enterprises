## 📋 Your Mission Tonight (60-90 minutes)

### STEP 1: Deploy to Netlify (10 min)
1. Go to: **https://app.netlify.com/signup**
2. Click **"Sign up with GitHub"**
3. After login, click **"Add new site"** → **"Import an existing project"**
4. Click **"Deploy with GitHub"**
5. Select repository: **-mbelmar-enterprises**
6. Click **"Deploy site"**
7. ✅ Wait 60 seconds - YOUR SITE IS LIVE!

**Your temporary URL:** `something-random-123456.netlify.app`

### STEP 2: Configure Form Email (5 min)
1. In Netlify dashboard → **Site settings** → **Forms**
2. Click **"Form notifications"**
3. Click **"Add notification"** → **"Email notification"**
4. Enter email: **admin@belmarenterprises.com**
5. Select event: **New form submission**
6. ✅ Click **"Save"**

### STEP 3: Buy Domain on GoDaddy (10 min)
1. Go to: **https://www.godaddy.com/**
2. Search for: **belmarenterprises.com** (or your choice)
3. Add to cart and checkout
4. ✅ Complete purchase

**Cost:** ~$10-20/year

### STEP 4: Connect Domain to Netlify (15 min)
1. In Netlify dashboard → **Domain settings**
2. Click **"Add custom domain"**
3. Enter: **belmarenterprises.com**
4. Click **"Verify"**
5. Click **"Set up Netlify DNS"**
6. ✅ Copy the 4 nameservers shown

**Example nameservers:**
```
dns1.p03.nsone.net
dns2.p03.nsone.net
dns3.p03.nsone.net
dns4.p03.nsone.net
```

### STEP 5: Update GoDaddy DNS (10 min)
1. Go to: **https://account.godaddy.com/products**
2. Find your domain → Click **"DNS"**
3. Scroll to **"Nameservers"**
4. Click **"Change"**
5. Select **"I'll use my own nameservers"**
6. Paste the 4 nameservers from Netlify
7. ✅ Click **"Save"**

### STEP 6: Wait for DNS (15-60 min)
- DNS propagation typically takes 15 min to 2 hours
- Check status: **https://dnschecker.org/**
- Enter your domain and check if it points to Netlify

☕ Take a break, check back in 30 minutes

### STEP 7: Enable HTTPS (2 min)
1. Once DNS is verified in Netlify (you'll see green checkmark)
2. Go to **Domain settings** → **HTTPS**
3. Netlify automatically provisions SSL certificate
4. Click **"Force HTTPS"** (this redirects http to https)
5. ✅ Done!

### STEP 8: Set Up Email (10 min)

**Option A: Email Forwarding (FREE - Easiest)**
1. In GoDaddy → Your domain → **Email**
2. Click **"Manage"** or **"Set up"** email forwarding
3. Forward: **admin@belmarenterprises.com** → your personal email
4. ✅ Done! Forms will forward to your email

**Option B: GoDaddy Email ($1.99/month)**
1. In GoDaddy → Buy **Email Essentials**
2. Create mailbox: **admin@belmarenterprises.com**
3. ✅ Full email account

**Option C: Google Workspace ($6/month)**
1. Sign up: **https://workspace.google.com**
2. Use Gmail with your domain
3. Most professional option

## 🆘 Quick Troubleshooting

**Netlify deploy failed?**
- Check GitHub repository has all files
- Click "Retry deploy" in Netlify

**Domain not working?**
- Wait 30 more minutes for DNS
- Check nameservers are correct
- Use https://dnschecker.org

**Form not working?**
- Check Netlify forms dashboard
- Verify email notification is set up
- Check spam folder

**HTTPS not ready?**
- DNS must propagate first (wait)
- Give it up to 24 hours
- Check Netlify domain settings
